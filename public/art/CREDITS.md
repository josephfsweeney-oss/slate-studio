Two were rejected and are worth recording so nobody puts them back. A "SOLD"
sign that carried a real estate agency's brand and logo: a living business does
not belong on political mail without its say-so. And a letter box that was not
an American one, which anybody in New Hampshire would have spotted.

Three of the nine were taken in New Hampshire: the State House in Concord, Main
Street in Nashua, and the village aerial, whose photographer works out of
Hillsborough. The other six are subject photographs and were chosen for the
issue, not the place.

Each file is fitted to 2200 px on the long edge and written as WebP at quality
0.86 by `build/pack-art.mjs`. That covers a full bleed on an 11 x 6 at about
200 dpi, under a veil and under type. Ask the printer to proof any piece that
runs one full bleed.

Unsplash asks applications that use its API to register a download when a photo
is taken. These were fetched from the image CDN rather than through the download
endpoint, which needs an API key this app does not hold. If the committee wants
that call made, it needs an Unsplash application key.
